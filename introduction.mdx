## How It Works

The Progress Pilot agent connects to your existing tools, reads context from your meetings and data systems, and takes action on your behalf — with full transparency into every step it takes.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect your tools">
    Link Google Workspace, Slack, Jira, and your data sources so the agent has the context it needs to work.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Define your scope">
    Configure which teams, meetings, and workflows the agent should monitor and act on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Let the agent work">
    The agent joins meetings, processes data, generates reports, and routes outputs to the right people automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and act">
    Get structured summaries, action items, and recommendations delivered to your preferred channels — ready to act on.
  </Step>
</Steps>
