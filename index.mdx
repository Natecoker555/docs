## Get Up and Running in 4 Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Sign up at [app.progresspilot.ai](https://app.progresspilot.ai) and configure your organization workspace.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your tools">
    Link Google Workspace, Slack, Jira, or any of the 30+ supported integrations so the agent has the context it needs.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run your first agent task">
    Invite the agent to a meeting or kick off an operations workflow — and get a full breakdown delivered to your team instantly.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure security and controls">
    Set up [data governance policies](/concepts/data-governance), [access controls](/configuration/security-controls), and [notification preferences](/configuration/notifications) to match your organization's requirements.
  </Step>
</Steps>

  Looking for a deeper walkthrough? The [Setup Guide](/getting-started/setup) covers workspace configuration, permissions, and your first agent task end to end.
</Tip>
